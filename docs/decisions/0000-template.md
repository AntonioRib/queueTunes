# NNNN — Short imperative title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forced this decision? What constraints, prior work, or user need is in play? Keep it short — a paragraph or two. Link to the relevant spec, todo, or issue.

## Decision

The choice, in one or two sentences. Present tense: "We use X" — not "We should" or "We might".

## Consequences

- **Good:** what this unlocks or simplifies.
- **Bad:** what this makes harder or forecloses.
- **Neutral:** notable side effects worth remembering.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A** — one-liner + why not.
- **Option B** — one-liner + why not.

## References

- Related ADRs, specs, external docs, PRs.
